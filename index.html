A product development process with no handoff gap.

Design leads the early weeks and engineering leads the build. Both teams review every milestone together.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Design Monks leads

    User interviews, competitor research and technical scoping. You get a discovery report, user personas and a costed roadmap both teams have signed off.

  2. 2

    Design

    Both studios

    Visual direction, UI screens and a design system, reviewed by engineers every week so nothing reaches development that can't be built.

  3. 3

    Build

    Dev Monks leads

    AI-assisted engineering in short sprints with weekly demos. Designers check each release against the system before it ships.

  4. 4

    Launch & grow

    Both studios

    QA, deployment and analytics, then conversion work and new features. Both studios stay on for post-launch support.

Questions founders ask us.

Can't find your answer? Send a brief and a lead will reply within one working day.

What does Monks do?

Monks is a product design and development studio. Through Design Monks and Dev Monks we offer UI/UX design, branding, website design, SaaS and mobile app development, and AI development under one contract.

Can I hire only one studio?

Yes. Hire Design Monks for design only, Dev Monks for engineering only, or Monks for the full team. Many clients start with one studio and add the other later.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a short discovery call we send a fixed quote or a monthly plan. Design Monks offers flexible monthly, quarterly and annual billing with no long-term lock-in.

How long does an MVP take to design and build?

We agree a timeline in discovery, once scope is clear. Because design and engineering plan together from week one, there is no gap between design sign-off and development start.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes. Our team is based in Dhaka and works with clients across the US, Europe, the Middle East and Asia, with regular calls and weekly demos.

Who owns the designs and code?

You do. Design files, source code and documentation are handed over in full, and both studios offer support after launch.

Tell us what you're building. A lead from each studio reads every brief and replies within one working day.
